The Approach

Why non-promotional racing?

No bonus bets. No promo turnover. Just market value. That one decision changes everything about how long you last in this game.

Account longevity

Promo-driven betting patterns get accounts restricted fast. Betting pure value at normal market prices keeps your bookmaker accounts alive far longer.

Independent of bookmakers

The system never relies on bookmaker incentives to generate its edge, so it keeps working when the promos dry up or your accounts stop receiving them.

Pure market value

We bet when the available price is longer than our modelled fair odds. That is the entire trigger. No price, no bet.

A repeatable process

Value betting is a long-term process you can repeat week after week, season after season. That's the foundation of sustainable racing betting.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I get access after I join?
Checkout runs through LaunchPass. Once payment is processed, your Discord account is automatically linked to your Racing membership and the private racing channels unlock in our server. You'll get a welcome message with onboarding instructions so you can be set up before the next race day.
When do the bets drop?
Early market bets are posted as soon as value is identified, typically the night before or early on race morning. On Saturdays, additional bets are released on race morning once track conditions, rail position and bias are confirmed. Hong Kong bets land ahead of the Happy Valley (Wednesday night) and Sha Tin (Sunday) meetings.
How much money do I need to start?
There's no fixed minimum. We recommend 1 unit equals roughly 1% of your bankroll, and every selection comes with recommended units. Whatever your bankroll, you scale the same staking proportionally. If you're unsure, the team helps you size correctly during onboarding.
What's the difference between the plans?
Nothing except the term. Every plan gets the same bets and the same channels. Weekly is $45 per week, monthly is $145 per month, and the Carnival Pass is a single $340 payment covering everything from 14 August to 14 November.
What happens when the Carnival Pass ends?
The pass is a one-off payment, so there's no auto-renewal. Access ends on 14 November, and if you want to keep going you can roll onto the weekly or monthly plan from there.
Do I need accounts with specific bookmakers?
The more books you have, the better the price you can take. Major Australian bookmakers (TAB, Sportsbet, Ladbrokes, bet365) cover most selections. Because the system is non-promotional, you don't need bonus bets or promo eligibility for it to work.
Is this suitable for beginners?
Yes, provided you're willing to follow instructions and manage your bankroll properly. Every bet comes with recommended units, so you always know how much to stake. This isn't a get-rich-quick service. It's a long-term, disciplined system.
What happens during a losing run?
Losing runs are part of racing, even with a genuine edge. The system works because we don't chase or change strategy after a bad week. We hold the line, keep staking controlled, track everything, and let the process play out over the full season.
Can I cancel my membership?
Weekly and monthly plans can be cancelled anytime through your LaunchPass portal, and access stays active until the end of your current billing period. The Carnival Pass is a single payment with no renewal, so there's nothing to cancel.
Where can I see the results?
Every bet is logged and graded in the member channels as it settles, and the racing record is published as it builds, wins and losses alike. FNF has published its AFL and NRL results publicly for five years, and racing is held to the same standard from day one.
